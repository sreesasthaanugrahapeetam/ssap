Our Guru Swamy

మన గురు స్వామి
Peetadhipati Manya Sri Ayyappa Indra Kumar Dev Guru Swamy

Manya Sri Ayyappa Indra Kumar Dev Guru Swamy

మాన్య శ్రీ అయ్యప్ప ఇంద్ర కుమార్ దేవ్ గురు స్వామి

Guru Swamy has been observing the sacred Ayyappa Deeksha for 27 continuous years — a deeply revered spiritual discipline undertaken in complete surrender and devotion to Lord Hari Hara Putra Ayyappa Swamy. This extraordinary commitment stands as a living testament to his unwavering faith at the Lord's lotus feet.

Under his divine guidance, Sree Sastha Anugraha Peetam (Regd. No. 11/2026) was established — a sacred space where devotees receive spiritual guidance, Ayyappa Deeksha initiation, and participate in Mandala Kala Deeksha Poojas every season.

"Lord Ayyappa's grace is for every soul. Walk the path of deeksha with a pure heart and the Lord's anugraha will guide you always. Swamiye Saranam Ayyappa."

About the Peetam

పీఠం గురించి

Sree Sastha Anugraha Peetam is a sacred spiritual centre consecrated to Lord Ayyappa Swamy — Sastha — the eternal celibate deity who embodies dharma, devotion, and boundless compassion. Born of the divine union of Lord Shiva and Mohini (Lord Vishnu's feminine form), Ayyappa Swamy is worshipped across all communities, transcending caste and creed.

Established with the divine blessings of Lord Sastha, this Peetam stands as a beacon of spiritual light — guiding devotees, conducting sacred rituals in full Vedic tradition, and nurturing the timeless bond between the devotee and the divine.

  • Daily poojas and archanas with authentic Vedic rituals
  • Ayyappa Deeksha guidance for Sabarimala pilgrims
  • Mandala and Makara Vilakku special celebrations
  • Harivarasanam and bhajan sessions for the community
  • Spiritual discourses and religious education

🕉 Presiding Deity

Lord Ayyappa Swamy (Sastha) — divine son of Shiva and Mohini, chief presiding deity of Sabarimala, embodiment of righteousness and grace

🙏 Our Mission

To nurture bhakti, propagate spiritual wisdom, and create a sanctuary of peace where every devotee finds the Lord's anugraha (grace)

Annadanam

అన్నదానం

అన్నదాన ప్రభువే — The Lord of Sacred Feeding

Annadanam — the gift of food — is the highest form of seva. At Sree Sastha Anugraha Peetam, feeding devotees is an act of worshipping the Lord himself, for Ayyappa Swamy dwells in every soul that receives prasadam with gratitude.

Poojas & Sevas

పూజలు మరియు సేవలు

Sacred rituals performed with full Vedic tradition, devotion and purity for the welfare of every devotee

🪷

Abhishekam

అభిషేకం

Sacred bathing of the Lord with Panchamrita — milk, curd, honey, ghee and sugar — accompanied by Vedic hymns

🌺

Archana

అర్చన

Offering flowers with 108 or 1008 divine names of Lord Ayyappa chanted with reverence and bhakti

🔥

Homam

హోమం

Sacred fire rituals — Ganapathi Homam, Ayyappa Homam, Mrityunjaya Homam — for health, prosperity and liberation

🪔

Deeparadhana

దీపారాధన

The evening lamp ceremony — the Lord is honoured with camphor flames, conch blows and devotional singing

🕉️

Padi Pooja

పడి పూజ

Sacred worship of the eighteen holy steps (Padi) with lamps, flowers, Ayyappa bhajans and Harivarasanam, performed at the Peetam or at devotees' homes

🎗️

Deeksha Guidance

దీక్ష మార్గదర్శనం

Complete guidance and initiation for the 41-day Sabarimala Deeksha, including Irumudi preparation and Vratha rules

🌙

Mandala Poojas

మండల పూజలు

Grand 41-day Mandala season celebrations with special rituals, annadanam and community gatherings

🏺

Ashta Kalasa Pooja

అష్ట కలశ పూజ

Eight sacred kalasams filled with holy water are invoked and offered to the Lord with Vedic mantras for peace, prosperity and well-being
